For the **Purdue AI Research Showcase** I helpedㄇ present a
teleoperation rig that pairs a **UR5** arm and a **dexterous hand** with an
**Apple Vision Pro**. The operator's hand and head motion map onto the robot,
turning natural human movement into precise robot action.

Beyond the live demo, the real value of the setup is as a **data-collection
pipeline**: it captures clean, high-quality demonstrations of delicate
manipulation that can train downstream imitation-learning policies, connecting
directly to my work on visuotactile manipulation.
