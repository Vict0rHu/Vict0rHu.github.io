**Bronze Prize** at the **Symtek Automation Asia Co. Smart Manufacturing Paper
Contest (2023)**, an industry paper competition judged on practical value to
the production floor.

My paper presented the AI real-time quality-prediction system I built as a
research assistant at NTNU. The pipeline samples live machine signals, screens
the high-impact factors with **Taguchi methods**, and feeds an **MLP** that
predicts product weight to within **0.2%**, accurate enough to catch quality
drift during the run instead of after inspection.

The same work became an academic paper at ARIS 2023. For this contest I rewrote
it for an industrial jury, arguing the results in terms of scrap rate,
inspection cost, and retrofit effort rather than model metrics. Learning to
present research to the people who would buy the machine was worth as much as
the prize itself.
