The research write-up behind my smart-manufacturing quality system, presented at
the **2023 Advanced Robotics and Intelligent Systems (ARIS) International
Conference** under the title *Smart Manufacturing with AI-Based Real-Time
Quality Prediction*.

The paper frames the engineering result in measurable terms: an AI model that
predicts product weight with only **0.2% deviation** from actual values, which
**reduced inspection time by over 80%** compared with the manual process. To
make it usable in practice, the system pairs a secure **database** with a
**real-time GUI**, giving both on-site operators and remote stakeholders instant
insight into production quality.

Presenting at ARIS was my first experience defending a system to an academic
audience, translating a working prototype into a result others could evaluate
and build on.
