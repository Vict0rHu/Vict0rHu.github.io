**First place** at the **National Taiwan Normal University Mechatronic Capstone
Competition (2023)**, the department's flagship hands-on contest, where teams are
judged on a complete working mechatronic system rather than a slide deck.

The winning entry was my
[**Smart-Manufacturing Quality Prediction System**](/portfolio/smart-mfg-quality-prediction/):
an AI model that predicts a product's weight to within **0.2%** straight from
live sensor signals, backed by real-time signal processing, a PyQt5 operator
dashboard, and an SQL traceability layer. What the judges rewarded was the
**end-to-end integration**: sensing hardware, the signal chain, machine learning,
and software brought together into one reliable system that held up under
competition conditions.

The same work went on to be published at the **2023 ARIS International
Conference** and recognized again with a Bronze Prize at the **Symtek Smart
Manufacturing Paper Contest**.
