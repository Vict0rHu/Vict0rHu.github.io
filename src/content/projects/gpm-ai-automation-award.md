**Honorable Mention** at the **Gallant Precision Machining Co. (GPM) AI
Automation Equipment Creation Award (2024)**, a corporate competition for
designs that put AI into real, buildable automation equipment.

Our team entered the AI-powered optical-inspection design that later grew into
my patent-pending **AOI device**: a photoelastic imaging front end, custom
fixturing and motion, and a deep-learning defect classifier, designed together
as one machine. As team lead I owned the system architecture across optics,
mechanism, firmware, and the inference pipeline, and packaged it into a
proposal a manufacturing jury could evaluate for cost, throughput, and
integration effort.

The award was early outside validation that the design could work as production
equipment, and it pushed the project toward the patent filing and the full
prototype.
