**Honorable Mention** at the **Gallant Precision Machining Co. (GPM) AI
Automation Equipment Creation Award, 2024**, an industry-sponsored competition
for designs that bring AI into real automation equipment.

The recognition reflects the same thread that runs through my project work:
pairing intelligent perception with practical, manufacturable hardware rather
than treating the algorithm and the machine as separate problems.
