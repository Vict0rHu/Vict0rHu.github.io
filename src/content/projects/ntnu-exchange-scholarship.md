Selected for the **NTNU Overseas Exchange Study Grant (2024)**, a university-wide
competitive scholarship awarded on academic record and a study proposal. The
grant funded a semester of my exchange at the **University of Nevada, Reno**.

I spent the exchange on coursework, picking advanced engineering electives
taught in a system I had never studied in. Beyond the classes themselves, the
semester taught me to adapt fast: a new grading culture, unfamiliar course
formats, and coursework and teamwork entirely in English.

The exchange confirmed that I wanted to build robots in the US, strengthened
the application that got me into Purdue's M.S. in Robotics, and made me
comfortable working far from home.
